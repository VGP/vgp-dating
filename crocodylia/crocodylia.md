# Crocodylia calibration

One single node, separating _Alligator_ and _Gavialis_ – Crocodylia. Generally dates to the Late/Upper Cretaceous, ~90Ma. TimeTree.org estimates a median age of 87Ma (72.9-101.0Ma) from 18 studies between 2004 and 2018. To this, we can add $XXX additional studies to generate a robust Type II calibration density.

The data are recorded in crocodylia_calibrations.tsv as an example.
