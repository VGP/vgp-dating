# Mammal calibrations

Our calibrations derive primarily from Upham et al. ([2019](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3000494)) and Álvarez-Carretero et al. ([2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04341-1)), with recent updates and revisions therein.

### Calibrations:

**Node 783:** Mammalia | 201.5-164.9Ma\
**Node calibrated:** Most recent common ancestor of monotremes (_Ornithorhynchus anatinus_), marsupials (_Monodelphis domestica_), and placentals (_Dasypus novemcinctus_)\
**Fossil taxa:** †_Ambondro mahabo_ (UA 10602) from the Isalo III Formation of the Mahajanga Basin in northwestern Madagascar ([Flynn et al. 1999](https://doi.org/10.1038/43420))\
**Minimum age:** Middle Jurassic (Bathonian), 164.9Ma\
**Minimum justification:** Specimen locality dated to Bathonian, top of which is 166.1 Ma, ± 1.2 Myr = 164.9 Ma ([Benton et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.26879/424))\
**Maximum age:** Jurassic-Triassic Boundary, 201.5Ma\
**Maximum justification:** The closest known relative of crown mammals, †_Hadrocodium_, dates to early Jurassic Sinemurian ([Luo et al. 2001](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1058476)), and other closely related mammaliaformes are known from Late Triassic ([Benton et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.26879/424))\
**Discussion:** Member of the monotreme clade Australosphenida from the middle Jurassic (Bathonian) of Madagascar ([Benton et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.26879/424); [Flynn et al. 1999](https://doi.org/10.1038/43420); [Luo et al. 2002](https://www.app.pan.pl/article/pdf/41/item/app47-001.html); [Luo et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10291))

---

**Node 947:** Monotremata | 113.0-15.97Ma\
**Node calibrated:** Most recent common ancestor of _Tachyglossus aculeatus_ and _Ornithorhynchus anatinus_\
**Fossil taxa:** †_Obdurodon dicksoni_ (QMF20568) from the Riversleigh World Heritage Area in Queensland, Australia ([Archer et al. 1992](https://catalogue.nla.gov.au/catalog/608888))\
**Minimum age:** Miocene, 15.97Ma\
**Minimum justification:** Oldest specimens found in northwestern Queensland in Faunal Zone B sites, which date to early Miocene ([Travouillon et al. 2006](https://doi.org/10.1080/03115510609506871))\
**Maximum age:** Early Cretaceous, 113.0Ma\
**Maximum justification:** Potential crown Monotremes are present in Argentina from ~61Ma, but the latest Australasian sites that lack any evidence of crown monotremes date to the Albian (Phillips 2015)\
**Discussion:** Stem member of Ornithorhynchidae from the early Miocene (Burdigalian) of northwestern Queensland, Australia ([Phillips 2015](https://doi.org/10.26879/490); [Luo et al. 2007](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature05627); [Phillips et al. 2009](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0904649106))

---

**Node 938:** Marsupialia | 131.3-47.6Ma\
**Node calibrated:** Most recent common ancestor of Paucituberculata (_Lestoros inca_), Didelphimorphia (_Monodelphis domestica_) and Australidelphia (_Dromiciops gliroides_)\
**Fossil taxa:** †_Djarthia murgonensis_ (QMF52748) from the Eocene Tingamarra Local Fauna at the Murgon fossil site in southeastern Queensland, Australia ([Godthelp et al. 1999](https://doi.org/10.1023%2FA%3A1020517808869))\
**Minimum age:** Eocene (Ypresian), 47.6Ma\
**Minimum justification:** The Murgon locality is correlated with the Ypresian marine stage ([Beck 2012](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00114-012-0953-x))\
**Maximum age:** Early Cretaceous (Barremian), 131.3Ma\
**Maximum justification:**  Therians such as †_Sinodelphys szalayi_ date to the Barremian ([Luo et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1090718); [Zhou et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01420)) and are diverse, but evidently are not metatherian ([Bi et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0210-3); [Beck 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-08419-5_35)). Diversity of therians and possible eutherians during the Barremian implies a similar antiquity for Metatheria to at least that time, but such taxa pre-date the radiation of crown Marsupialia.\
**Discussion:** A crown marsupial from early Eocene of Murgon, Australia ([Benton et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.26879/424); Beck [2012](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00114-012-0953-x), [2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-08419-5_35))
