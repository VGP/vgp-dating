# Turtle calibrations

Turtles have a dense and detailed fossil record that has made them particularly amenable to time-scaled phylogenetic analysis using fossil calibrations, reviewed in detail by [Joyce et al. (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1666/12-149) and references therein. That study provided 22 well-constrained node ages to date the turtle tree, which were corroborated and partially updated by [Selvatti et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ympev.2023.107773), who also provided a summary of ages for Testudines suitable for use as likelihood checkpoints. Given our modest sampling of turtles, only 9 of the 22 constraints are applicable here, which we list below as our primary calibrations.

### Calibrations:

**Node 732:** Testudines | 157.3–252.0Ma\
**Node calibrated:** Last common ancestor of the clade encompassing all extant turtles.\
**Fossil taxa:** _Notoemys oxfordiensis_ (MNHNCu P 3209)\
**Minimum age:** 157.3Ma\
**Minimum justification:** As per [Cadena and Joyce (2015)](https://doi.org/10.3374/014.056.0101), the oldest crown testudine from the Viñales locality, Pinar del Río Province, Cuba; Jagua Vieja Member, Jagua Formation, Oxfordian, Late Jurassic ([de la Fuente and Iturralde-Vinent 2001](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-paleontology/article/new-pleurodiran-turtle-from-the-jagua-formation-oxfordian-of-western-cuba/33E57BAE07B114094E5C322E1D4F190F)). List of synapomorphies given by [Selvatti et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ympev.2023.107773).\
**Maximum age:** 252.0Ma\
**Maximum justification:** Maximum age based on the lower boundary of the Triassic.\
**Discussion:** The minimum age of 157.3Ma for crown Testudines is generally used as a maximum age for other calibrations in the absence of more precise constraints. As noted by [Selvatti et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ympev.2023.107773): "_Yehguia tatsuensis_ stems from the same age (Oxfordian) and also serves as a prior for this node."


**Node 747:** Pleurodira | 125.0–157.3Ma\
**Node calibrated:** Last common ancestor of the clade encompassing all extant pleurodiran turtles.\
**Fossil taxa:** _Atolchelys lepida_ ()\
**Minimum age:** 125.0Ma\
**Minimum justification:** Based the oldest crown pleurodiran from the Barremian of the Cretaceous (121.4–125.77Ma), with a list of synapomorphies given by [Selvatti et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ympev.2023.107773).\
**Maximum age:** 157.3Ma\
**Maximum justification:** Maximum age based on the oldest pan-pleurodiran _Notoemys oxfordiensis_ from the Oxfordian of the Jurassic (154.8–161.5Ma).\
**Discussion:** [Selvatti et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ympev.2023.107773) assign this fossil to the less-inclusive clade Pelumedusoides, but our taxon sampling is not detailed enough to capture this crown group. As result, we calibrate its stem age instead, which is equivalent to the crown group Pleurodira.
