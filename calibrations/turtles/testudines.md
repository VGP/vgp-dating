# Turtle calibrations

Turtles have a dense and detailed fossil record that has made them particularly amenable to time-scaled phylogenetic analysis using fossil calibrations, reviewed in detail by [Joyce et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1666/12-149) and references therein. That study provided 22 well-constrained node ages to date the turtle tree, which were corroborated and partially updated by [Selvatti et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ympev.2023.107773), who also provided a summary of ages for Testudines suitable for use as likelihood checkpoints. Given our modest sampling of turtles, only some of the 22 constraints are applicable here, which we list below as Type III calibrations along with the assembled age-estimates for a likelihood checkpoint on Testudines.

### Type III Calibrations:
**732:** Testudines: 157.3–252.0Ma. Based on the oldest crown testudine _Notoemys oxfordiensis_ from the Oxfordian of the Jurassic (154.8–161.5Ma), with a maximum based on the maximum age of the Triassic. The minimum age of 157.3Ma is generally used as a maximum age for other calibrations in the absence of more precise constraints.

**747:** Pleurodira: 125.0–155.6Ma. Based the oldest crown pleurodiran _Atolchelys lepida_ from the Barremian of the Cretaceous (121.4–125.77Ma), with a maximum based on the oldest pan-pleurodiran _Notoemys oxfordiensis_ from the Oxfordian of the Jurassic (154.8–161.5Ma).

**733:** Cryptodira: 157.3–200.2Ma. Based on the oldest crown cryptodiran _Yehguia tatsuensis_ from the Oxfordian of the Jurassic (154.8–161.5Ma), with a maximum based on the minimum age of the Triassic.

**734:** Durocryptodira: 88.6–157.3Ma. Based on the oldest crown durocryptodiran _Lindholmemys elegans_, likely stem Testudinoidea, from the Turonian of the Cretaceous (89.8–93.9Ma).

**744:** Testuguria: 50.3–157.3Ma. Based on the oldest crown testgurian _Hadrianus majusculus_, likely stem Testudinidae, from the Wasatchian of the Eocene (50.3–55.4Ma).

**746:** Emydidae: 32.0–157.3Ma. Based on the oldest crown emydid _Chrysemys antiqua_ from the Orellan of the Oligocene (32.0–33.7Ma).

**735:** Americhelydia: 70.0–157.3Ma. Based on the oldest crown americhelyid _Toxochelys latiremys_, likely stem Chelonioidea, from the Campanian of the Cretaceous (72.2–83.6Ma).

**742:** Chelydroidea: 70.0–157.3Ma. Based on the oldest crown chelydroid SEPCP 9/493, likely stem Chelydridae, from the Campanian of the Cretaceous (72.2–83.6Ma).
