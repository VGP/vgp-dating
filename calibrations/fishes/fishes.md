This placement was corroborated by the inclusion of lophiiform terminals by [Arcila & Tyler (2017)](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/abs/10.1098/rspb.2017.1771) and [Troyer et al. 2022](https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.2122486119).
