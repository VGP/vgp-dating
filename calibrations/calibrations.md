-Based on _Eodiscoglossus santonjae_ (Cretaceous: Barremian, 121.4-125.8Ma), supported by 2 total-evidence analyses. **This is younger than some internal calibrations, and not particularly close to estimated ages.**

## [Squamates](./squamata/squamata.md):

**748:** Squamata: 166.0–215.0Ma\
-based on the earliest crown squamates _Eichstaettisaurus, Helioscopus,_ and _Paramacellodus_ sp., from the middle Jurassic.

**749:** Unidentata: 166.0–205.0Ma\
-based on the earliest stem Scincoidea (Paramacellodidae: _Paramacellodus_ sp.), from the middle Jurassic..

**767:** Toxicofera: 166.0–190.0Ma\
-based on the earliest known snake _Eophis_ and earliest putative crown toxicoferan _Breugnathair_, from the middle Jurassic.

**769:** Endoglyptodonta: 20.0–66.0Ma\
-based on _Vipera_ cf. _aspis_, the oldest crown Viperinae from the early Miocene.

**771:** Natricidae: 13.8–33.9Ma\
-based on _Natrix_ aff. _longivertebrata_, the oldest crown Natricinae.

**774:** Stem Booidea: 48.0–100.5Ma\
-based on _Eoconstrictor_ and _Rageryx_, the earliest stem booids from the middle Eocene.

**777:** Pleurodonta: 48.0–120.0Ma\
-based on _Geiseltaliellus_ and _Babibasiliscus_, the oldest crown pleurodontans from the early Eocene.  

**778:** Chamaeleonidae: 20.0–66.0Ma\
-based on the oldest crown chameleonid _Calumma benovskyi_, from the early Miocene.

**779:** Anguimorpha: 70.0–150.0Ma\

**780:** Anguidae: 33.9–100.5Ma\

## [Turtles](./turtles/testudines.md):

**732:** Testudines: 157.3–252.0Ma\
-based on the oldest crown testudine _Notoemys oxfordiensis_ from the Oxfordian of the Jurassic (154.8–161.5Ma).

**747:** Pleurodira: 125.0–155.6Ma\
-based the oldest crown pleurodiran _Atolchelys lepida_ from the Barremian of the Cretaceous (121.4–125.77Ma).

**733:** Cryptodira: 157.3–200.2Ma\
-based on the oldest crown cryptodiran _Yehguia tatsuensis_ from the Oxfordian of the Jurassic (154.8–161.5Ma).

**734:** Durocryptodira: 88.6–157.3Ma\
-based on the oldest crown durocryptodiran _Lindholmemys elegans_, likely stem Testudinoidea, from the Turonian of the Cretaceous (89.8–93.9Ma).

**744:** Testuguria: 50.3–157.3Ma\
-based on the oldest crown testgurian _Hadrianus majusculus_, likely stem Testudinidae, from the Wasatchian of the Eocene (50.3–55.4Ma).

**746:** Emydidae: 32.0–157.3Ma\
-based on the oldest crown emydid _Chrysemys antiqua_ from the Orellan of the Oligocene (32.0–33.7Ma).

**735:** Americhelydia: 70.0–157.3Ma\
-based on the oldest crown americhelyid _Toxochelys latiremys_, likely stem Chelonioidea, from the Campanian of the Cretaceous (72.2–83.6Ma).

**742:** Chelydroidea: 70.0–157.3Ma\
-based on the oldest crown chelydroid SEPCP 9/493, likely stem Chelydridae, from the Campanian of the Cretaceous (72.2–83.6Ma).

**736:** Chelonioidea: 48.4–157.3Ma\
-based on the oldest known chelonioid _Eosphargis breineri_, likely stem Dermochelyidae, from the Ypresian of the Eocene (48.07–56.0Ma).


## [Crocodilians:](./crocodylia/crocodylia.md) 

**731:** Crocodylia: 77.9-113.2Ma\
-Based on _Leidyosuchus canadensis_ (Cretaceous: Campanian, 77.9–72.1Ma), supported by 8 total-evidence analyses.

**Likelihood checkpoints:**\
Molecular estimates from 23 studies between 2004 and 2021 yield a median of 87.4Ma with a range from 70.9–110.2Ma.\
Total-evidence estimates from 6 studies between 2017 and 2025 estimate a median of 97.4Ma with a range from 88.3–107.9Ma.
