# Amphibia calibrations

There are relatively few amphibians sampled, leaving Type III & IV calibrations primarily to a few frog groups, mostly in Neobatrachia. 

For a Type IV calibration, the total-evidence analysis of [Chen et al. 2016](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep19209) estimated _Eodiscoglossus santonjae_ in the crown of Anura, corroborated by the morphological re-description by [Baez and Gomez 2016](https://ri.conicet.gov.ar/handle/11336/60180) and re-analysis by [Gao and Chen 2017](https://bioone.org/journals/American-Museum-Novitates/volume-2017/issue-3876/3876.1/A-New-Crown-Group-Frog-Amphibia--Anura-from-the/10.1206/3876.1.short). This taxon dates to the Barremian, ~121.4–125.8Ma. Other putative crown-group anurans are known from similar ages as summarized in [Gomez and Andres 2019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1631068319301320), but these are not as well-documented. Consequently, we place a minimum bound of 121.4Ma on the MRCA of Anura, with a maximum of 252Ma for the maximum age of the Triassic. This is not particularly close to most existing times, which generally date to the Triassic.

For a Type III calibration, a more ancient constraint supported by a few recent studies is _Rhadinosteus parvus_ from the Kimmeridgian of the late Jurassic (150.8-155.7Ma), which forms the sister lineage of _Rhinophrynus_ (Rhinophrynidae) in the time-scaled morphological parsimony analysis of [Agnolin et al. 2020](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0895981120301462) and the supertree analysis of [Perez-Ben et al. 2024](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/paleobiology/article/frog-limbs-in-deep-time-is-jumping-locomotion-at-the-roots-of-the-anuran-bauplan/AC8EC495E05D51667CAAE5B18FAD1E67). As a result, we place a minimum of 150.8Ma on the MRCA of _Rhinophrynus_ and _Xenopus_ (Dorsipares _sensu_ [Dubois et al. 2021](https://www.mapress.com/mt/article/view/38637)), with a maximum of 252.0 for the maximum age of the Triassic. **This constraint could also be treated as a stem dorsiparid and placed on the MRCA of Geobatrachia.**

There are well-documented times for likelihood checkpoints at the base of Amphibia. Previous estimates for Lissamphibia (Gymnophiona + Batrachia) with and without _Gerobatrachus_ (depending on its placement as a stem batrachian) were previously compiled by [Kligman et al. (2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05646-5). We summarize those here to obtain a 95% CI from the quantiles. Those authors also summarized the published evidence for Batrachia (Salientia + Urodela) and Gymnophiona (Rhinatrematidae + Stegokrotaphia*), which we analyzed in the same manner:

Lissamphibia with _Gerobatrachus_: 278–361Ma (Median 320Ma)\
Lissamphibia without _Gerobatrachus_: 316–363Ma (Median 326Ma)\
Batrachia: 219–348Ma (Median 292Ma)\
Gymnophiona: 91.6–259Ma (Median 216Ma)

**TO ADD:** Salamandroidea (Ambystomatidae + Salamandridae)

*This is Plesiophiona + Pseudophiona in the nomenclature of Dubois et al. 2021
