# Squamata calibrations

Squamates have a high density of well-preserved fossils, reviewed by [Simões and Pyron 2021](https://bioone.org/journals/bulletin-of-the-museum-of-comparative-zoology/volume-163/issue-2/0027-4100-163.2.47/THE-SQUAMATE-TREE-OF-LIFE/10.3099/0027-4100-163.2.47.short). Results from total-evidence analyses have been relatively consistent across most recent studies, providing a plethora of potential Type IV calibrations. In contrast, our limited sampling of squamates limits this applicability somewhat. Here, we primarily rely on the most recent total-evidence analysis of [Simões et al. 2026](https://academic.oup.com/sysbio/advance-article/doi/10.1093/sysbio/syaf067/8266822) and references therein, using the nomenclature of [Burbrink et al. 2020](https://academic.oup.com/sysbio/article/69/3/502/5573126) for node names. We can add another Type IV calibration from the recent time-calibrated morphological phylogenetic analyses analyses of [Benson et al. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09566-y). A secondary source of Type III calibrations is [Title et al. 2024](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adh2449) (their Table S1) and references therein. We place the maximum age of the Triassic (252.0Ma) as the maximum age of most constraints, consistent the earliest age for stem-group Squamata subtending _Megachirella_ from Simōes et al. 2026.


### Type IV Calibrations:

**748:** Squamata: 167.2–252.0Ma. This calibration is based on the earliest stem Gekkota taxon _Bellairsia gracilis_ (Paramacellodidae), from the Bathonian of the late Jurassic (165.3–168.2Ma) analyzed by Simões et al. 2026. **This taxon is not confidently placed on the Gekkota stem, but this is a very safe minimum age for the crown group Squamata. Similarly-aged stem gekkotans were analyzed by [Meyer et al. 2023](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rspb/article/290/2011/20232284/104268/A-Morrison-stem-gekkotan-reveals-gecko-evolution).**

**751:** Laterata: 130.2–252.0Ma. This calibration is based on the earliest stem Teiiodea taxon _Dalinghosaurus longidigitus_ , from the Barremian of the early Cretaceous (128.0–132.0Ma) analyzed by Simões et al. 2026.

**767:** Toxicofera: 167.0–252.0Ma. This calibration is based on the earliest potential crown Toxicofera taxon _Breugnathair elgolensis_ (Parviraptoridae), from the Bathonian of the middle Jurassic (165.3–168.2Ma), based on Datasets 2 and 3 from Benson et al. 2025. **This placement has not been corroborated by other total-evidence analyses, but the timescale is highly congruent with most recent estimates, including Title et al. 2024 and Simões et al. 2026.**


### Type III Calibrations:

**749:** Unidentata: 170.0–252.0Ma. This calibration is based on the earliest Paramacellodidae (stem Scincoidea), from the Bajocian of the Jurassic (168.2–170.9Ma).

**779:** Anguimorpha: 144.0–252.0Ma. This calibration is based on the oldest crown anguimorph _Dorsetisaurus purbeckensis_, from the Tithonian of the Jurassic (143.1–149.2Ma).
