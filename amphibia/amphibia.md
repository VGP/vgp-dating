# Amphibia calibrations

There are relatively few amphibians sampled, leaving Type III & IV calibrations primarily to a few frog groups, mostly in Neobatrachia. In contrast, we might be better served with Type I & II calibrations at the root. 

Previous estimates for Lissamphibia (Gymnophiona + Batrachia) with and without _Gerobatrachus_ (depending on its placement as a stem batrachian) were previously compiled by [Kligman et al. (2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05646-5). We summarize those here as a normal density to obtain a 95% CI. Those authors also summarized the published evidence for Batrachia (Salientia + Urodela), which we analyzed in the same manner.
